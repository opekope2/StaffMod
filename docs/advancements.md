# Staff Mod Advancements

How many references can you find?

## Bazeboze

Hit a [Bat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bat) using a Staff with [Netherite Block](staff.md#netherite-block).

## Bulldozer

Break a block using a Staff with [Diamond Block](staff.md#diamond-block) or a Staff with [Netherite Block](staff.md#netherite-block).

## Coming down

Take fall damage while using a Staff with [Campfire/Soul Campfire](staff.md#campfire-soul-campfire).

## Coronavirus

Die while wearing [Crown of King Orange](items.md#crown-of-king-orange). Popping a [Totem of Undying](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Totem_of_Undying) also counts.

## Trust me, I'm an engineer!

??? hidden "This is a hidden advancement"
    Throw a [Cake](staff.md#cake) and a [TNT](staff.md#tnt) in a way that the TNT bumps into the cake and explodes, bumping the cake. The advancement is granted when the cake hits a player.

## Flying into space like a rocket

Use a Staff with [Campfire/Soul Campfire](staff.md#campfire-soul-campfire) to fly above build limit.

## Get pranked LOL

Get thrown with a [Cake](staff.md#cake).

## Infinite Power!

Obtain a Staff.

## Juggler

Hit an [impact TNT](staff.md#tnt) 3 times with an item enchanted with [Silk Touch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silk_Touch).

## Learning to fly

Use a Staff with [Campfire/Soul Campfire](staff.md#campfire-soul-campfire) to fly.

## Meet the Engineer

??? hidden "This is a hidden advancement"
    Throw a [Cake](staff.md#cake) and a [TNT](staff.md#tnt) in a way that the TNT bumps into the cake and explodes, bumping the cake. The advancement is granted when the cake hits you.

## Meteor

Reach Mach 0.5 while falling down.

## Prankster

Throw a [Cake](staff.md#cake) at someone.
