# Staff Mod Enchantments

## Distant Detonation

Hold the use key to throw a [TNT](staff.md#tnt) from a Staff with TNT. Release the key to detonate the TNT.

## Power Charge

Fire explosive fireballs from a Staff with [Magma Block](staff.md#magma-block).
Fire blue skuls from a Staff with [Wither Skeleton Skull](staff.md#wither-skeleton-skull).

## Quick Draw

Reduces cooldown and [block pickup](staff.md#no-item) speed.

## Rapid Fire

Enables firing multiple projectiles using the use key. Staffs enchanted with higher levels of Rapid Fire shoot more projectiles/second.

## Spectre

Adds `enchantment level * 10s` of [Glowing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowing) to the attacked entity, if attacked using a Staff with [Bell](staff.md#bell).
